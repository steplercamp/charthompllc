Support

Up Support

If you need help with Up, have a bug report, or have questions about your account, contact support and we’ll reply as soon as we can.

Contact Support

Email: support@charthompllc.com

Helpful Details to Include

  • Your phone number or account identifier used with Up.
  • A short description of the problem.
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • Your device model and iOS version.
  • Screenshots, if relevant.

Account and Privacy Requests

For account deletion, privacy questions, or data requests, email support@charthompllc.com and include enough detail for us to locate your account.
